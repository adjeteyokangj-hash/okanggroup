About

About OkangGroup

OkangGroup is a digital product and software group focused on building useful platforms for real-world operations. We develop our own products and also create systems for selected clients.

Our mission is to help organisations improve how they manage customers, bookings, operations, communication, reporting and growth — with software that is practical, secure and ready for everyday use.

Our approach is consultative and delivery-led: understand the problem, plan the system, build carefully, test thoroughly, then support the platform as the organisation grows.

Process

How we work

A clear path from first conversation to a live system — and support after launch.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We listen to the business or organisation need, constraints and success criteria before recommending an approach.

  2. 2

    Planning

    We structure features, users, permissions, workflows and the long-term direction of the platform.

  3. 3

    Development

    We build practical web, mobile and backend systems with secure access and clear ownership of data.

  4. 4

    Testing

    We verify the system thoroughly before release and refine it based on real use.

  5. 5

    Ongoing support

    We support live systems and plan improvements as your organisation grows.
